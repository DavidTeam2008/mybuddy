对译 App Icon — Four Candidates

Static render comparison for the on-device translation app 对译. Each candidate is a 1024×1024 opaque deep-ink square rendered from its SVG source at 1024 / 180 / 120 / 60 px, plus simulated iOS home-tile and tinted treatments. All four share the settled canvas, palette, and small-size hierarchy below. Verdicts remain pending.

Settled design decisions

Canvas & background

1024×1024 full-bleed, fully opaque deep ink (#0B0C10) with a faint #14161C radial lift. No transparency: every export is RGB with no alpha channel.

Palette

#0B0C10 #14161C #E8B86D #C98F3F #FFF4E0 #7EC8C3

Mark language

Exactly two speech bubbles and one bidirectional exchange arrow. Amber is speech / translation; warm white is the arrow and rims; the cool teal reflection stays at 10–12% opacity and is never load-bearing.

Small-size hierarchy

Silhouette first, arrow second. Gradients, speculars, pane lines, tails, and reflections are decorative and drop away; the arrow geometry and bubble silhouette are never simplified. Verified at 60 px and 40 px.

Safe area & masking

All mark geometry sits inside the centered 819×819 safe area, so the iOS corner mask never clips a bubble or an arrow tip and no critical detail lives in a corner.

Asset sizes

1024 px store / marketing master, 180 px @3x home screen, 120 px @2x, 60 px @2x settings and notifications. Candidate SVGs live one directory up in ../candidates/.

Mirrored Bubbles

mirrored-bubbles
Verdict pending
Concept
Two equal amber glass speech bubbles mirror each other across the vertical axis and kiss at top and bottom, their enclosed central gap cut into a clean vertical double-headed exchange arrow.
Arrow construction
The gap is a single negative-space vertical double arrow cut by a mask through the two mirrored bubbles (left body 228..512 x 284..756, right mirrored, x=512 axis): top tip (512,364), bottom tip (512,676); each head 232 wide (barbs at x=396/628, bases y=468/572); shaft 88 wide (x=468..556) by 104 tall; 36 px amber bridges separate the tips from the r44 kiss notches above and below, so the arrow reads as a closed clean cut, not an open notch. A 5 px #FFF4E0 rim at 30% opacity traces the cut, and the 2.64:1 head-to-shaft ratio keeps the chevrons bold.
Small-size plan
At 60 px the arrow is about 19 px tall, 14.5 px at the heads and 5.2 px at the shaft, an ink hole with amber flanks on both sides; the bubble mass is about 33x28 px, and tails, seam and notch are decorative only. Gradients, the inset pane line and the 10% cool reflection are dropped first at small sizes; the arrow geometry and bubble silhouette are never simplified. Verified legible at 256 px, 60 px and 40 px renders.

Overlapping Lens

overlapping-lens
Verdict pending
Concept
Two rounded speech bubbles overlap into a luminous lens, and a carved bidirectional arrow turns their shared negative space into a single act of translation.
Arrow construction
One continuous double-headed arrow is carved from the lens as true negative space: a 60 px shaft with 156 px tall heads, rotated 5.7 degrees to follow the lens tilt and held about 10 px inside the rim. The two opposing arrowheads share one shaft and are unified into one bold glyph so the bidirectional read survives at 40 px; the mask cuts through every glass layer to the ink canvas, leaving a 5 px warm-white edge light around the cut.
Small-size plan
At 60 px and 40 px the mark reduces to two offset round bubbles, a bright overlap lens, and one clear double arrow. Tails, soft rims, speculars, and inner glows disappear without changing the read; the arrow remains about 17 px / 11.6 px long with a 3.5 px / 2.3 px shaft, so no recognizable element depends on fine detail.

Asymmetric Listen

asymmetric-listen
Verdict pending
Concept
A large amber speech bubble speaks while a small clear, warm-white-rimmed glass bubble listens, and the gap between them holds one bold bidirectional exchange arrow.
Arrow construction
One single closed polygon path (shaft plus both chevron heads, 260 units tip-to-tip and 164 units across the heads) drawn as the last layer, rotated 45.8 degrees and centered on the gap midpoint at (563, 548). Each tip lands about 4 units inside the facing bubble edge, so the arrow reads as the negative space between the two bubbles while physically touching both. Head length 62, head half-width 82, shaft half-height 38; filled with a subtle warm-white gradient (#FFF4E0 100% to 86%), no outline and no filter.
Small-size plan
The mark is deliberately three-value at small sizes: bright amber mass, near-white arrow, thin warm ring on dark glass. At 40 px the speaker is about 14x12 px of solid #E8B86D, the arrow is about 10 px long with a 3 px shaft and 6.4 px heads, and the listener is about 9x8 px with a 0.8 px warm-white rim over a dark warm-tinted fill, holding its silhouette via the ring plus its outward tail. Speculars, bloom and the cool reflection are supportive only; no detail is load-bearing. Verified by down-renders at 256 px, 60 px and 40 px.

Waveform Exchange

waveform-exchange
Verdict pending
Concept
A live sound waveform runs through two interlocking speech bubbles and resolves into a bold bidirectional exchange arrow, so the act of translating reads as one continuous signal.
Arrow construction
A single 544 px long bidirectional arrow is cut as negative space through both bubbles with an SVG mask down to the ink canvas, rotated -8 degrees and centered at (482, 500). Each head is 132 px long and 180 px tall with a 56 px shaft; the shaft is a two-cycle sine (140 px period, 26 px amplitude, 56 px stroke) whose ends terminate inside the 140 px deep arrowhead bases, so no wave-to-shaft joint is visible. A 14 px warm-white edge light outlines the cut.
Small-size plan
At 60 px and 40 px the mark reduces to two overlapping round speech bubbles and one bold dark double arrow; tails, speculars, cool reflections and the edge light drop away. The arrow spans about 32 px at 60 px (3.3 px shaft, 10.5 px heads) and the waveform flattens optically into a single bold stroke, so the bidirectional read never depends on wave detail.
